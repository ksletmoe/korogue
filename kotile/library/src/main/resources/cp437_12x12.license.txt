Derivation: the 256 CP437 glyphs (8x16 px each) were rendered from the pcface
bitmap data and resized per-glyph to 12x12 px (Lanczos, with a smoothstep
contrast pass to firm up edges), then laid out as a 16x16 CP437 grid (code
point 0 at top-left, row-major) to match kotile's Font loader. The sheet
carries glyph coverage in its alpha channel (RGB is white throughout), so it
composites cleanly over any cell background; load it with keyColor = null
(the default in Fonts.cp437_12x12()).
