fonts/UbuntuMono-R.ttf

Source:  Ubuntu Mono (Regular), by Canonical Ltd. — https://design.ubuntu.com/font
         A monospaced typeface with broad CP437 coverage — box-drawing
         (U+2500–257F), block elements and shades (U+2580–259F, incl. U+2588
         full block), Greek, and the accented ranges a code-page-437 grid needs.
         It is the default TrueType face for the tier-3 freetype glyph source
         (FreeTypeGlyphSource / Fonts.ubuntuMono), rasterized at the cell pixel
         size (ADR-0036 tier 3, krogue-9x7.2). A handful of CP437 symbols the
         face does not carry (e.g. ☺ U+263A, ⌂ U+2302, ∩ U+2229, ≡ U+2261,
         ⌠⌡ U+2320/1, ⁿ U+207F, ■ U+25A0, ₧ U+20A7) fall back to the face's
         .notdef glyph.
