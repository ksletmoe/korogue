PortalZoneAdjacency

class PortalZoneAdjacency(gameWorld: GameWorld) : ZoneAdjacency(source)

The default ZoneAdjacency: a zone's neighbours are the distinct Portal.targetZoneIds of portal entities standing in that zone — i.e. wherever a Portal leads, that zone counts as adjacent. Reads gameWorld live, so it tracks portals added or removed after construction (no snapshot is cached).
